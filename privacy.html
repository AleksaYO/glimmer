Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Short version: Glimmer does not collect your personal data.

The app

  • Glimmer works fully on your Mac. It does not send analytics, usage data or crash reports anywhere.
  • Your activation code is checked offline and stored only on your Mac, in the app's settings.
  • To draw snow on top of your windows and the Dock, the app reads the position and size of windows on your screen. It does not read window contents, and this information never leaves your Mac.

Purchases

Payments are handled by third-party payment providers: Paddle.com (our reseller and Merchant of Record for card, PayPal and Apple Pay / Google Pay orders) and NOWPayments for cryptocurrency. They process the payment details you give them under their own privacy policies; we don't receive or store your card or wallet details. To issue your activation code we keep a record linking the code to the payment ID.

The website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It doesn't use cookies, ads or analytics. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Email

If you write to us, we use your email address only to reply to you.
