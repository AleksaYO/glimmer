FAQ

Questions

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once and the license is yours forever.

Will it slow down my Mac?

Glimmer uses about 10% CPU at 24 FPS. You can lower the frame rate, intensity and particle limit at any time, or pause it with one click.

Which Macs are supported?

Any Mac with macOS 13 Ventura or later — both Apple Silicon and Intel.

Is it in the App Store?

No — Glimmer is downloaded from this site. That’s why macOS asks you to confirm the first launch.

Can I use my own wallpapers?

Yes. Every effect can have its own picture, or keep the bundled 4K wallpapers.
